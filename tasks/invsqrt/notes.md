## 1. What the reference does (paper arXiv:2610.23832 §III.A, Eqs. 13–31)

`x` is 22 bits (`n_p = 10`, `width = 2n_p + 2`), `1 ≤ x ≤ 3,133,452`.

1. **Normalize**: count leading zero *pairs* `s` (unary, 10 flag qubits, one `init_and` per flag) and shift
   `x` in place by `2s` with flag-controlled lane rotations (CSWAPs), so `M = x_norm/2^20 ∈ [1,4)` and
   `1/√x = 2^(s−10)/√M`.
2. **Initial guess** `y0 ≈ 1/√M` (17 fractional bits): 4 cubic polynomials (intervals `[1,1.5) [1.5,2) [2,3) [3,4)`,
   coefficients from the paper's Ref. [29]) selected by the top 3 bits of `x_norm`. Coefficients are XORed in by
   Clifford `gf2_iadd` (free), then Horner with `delta = M − {1,1.5,2,3}` truncated to 14 bits:
   `r1 = c2 − T(c3·δ)`, `r2 = c1 − T(r1·δ)`, `y0 = c0 − T(r2·δ)`. Each `T(·)` is a column-truncated
   schoolbook multiply (`_add_row`: `init_and` partial products into scratch → `iadd` slice → `del_and` free).
3. **One Newton step** `p = T(y0·(3 − T(M·T(y0²)))) ≈ 2/√M` (square 28 bits, product 28+2 bits, result 29+1 bits),
   then **denormalize** `y1 = p·2^s` by 10 flag-controlled left rotations of a 40-bit register.
4. **Phase**: one `Z_POW` per bit of `y1` (F = 40 fractional bits of a turn): bit 39 → Z, bit 38 → S (free),
   bit 37 → T (1 T), bits 0..36 → 37 priced rotations.
5. **Uncompute** 1–3 in reverse (adders become subtractors, `init_and`→`del_and`).

Measured primitive costs (static, kickmix 0.1 builder): `iadd` of n bits = n−1 Toffolis + n−1 workspace qubits

(+k workspace), `del_lookup` ≈ 2^(k/2)·2.5 Toffolis (k=8: 254 + 25; k=9: 510 + 41; k=6: 62 + 10).

## 3. Slack in the budget

* **Phase tolerance**: `tol(x) = 2^-24/√x` turns. The circuit's worst relative error is 2^-25.07 = **0.477 tol**
  (x = 2,588,411); it is deterministic (equals the classical model to the bit), so the 52 % headroom is real,
  not a seed artefact.
* **The binding error is the cubic initial guess, not fixed point**: with the reference coefficients and 14-bit δ,
  `y0` has relative error 2^-12.75; one Newton step turns e into ≈1.5e², i.e. 2^-24.9 in exact arithmetic
  (fixed point happens to land at 2^-25.07). A near-minimax cubic on `[1,1.5]` reaches 2^-13.98 and on `[1.5,2]`
  2^-16 in float, so **re-fitting the four cubics for the fixed-point pipeline could buy ~1–2 bits of y0 accuracy →
  2–4 bits at the output**, which would then pay for the precision cuts below.
* **Rotation budget**: `eps = π·min_margin/n_rot` is set by the smallest absolute margin (large x). Each rotation
  costs 27.28 T = 6.82 Toffoli-eq; the eps term is logarithmic, so halving the margin costs only +0.56 T per
  rotation (+5 Toffoli-eq total) while removing one rotation saves 6.8.
* **Precision knobs of `Precision()` (classical model, `err/tol` must stay < 1; Toffoli deltas from rebuilding)**:

  | change | err/tol | Toffolis | Q |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | baseline (14,17,28,28,29) | 0.477 | 7,735 | 260 |
  | delta_bits 13 | 0.602 | −114 | 0 |
  | delta_bits 12 | 1.012 ✗ | −234 | 0 |
  | coef_bits 16 | 1.340 ✗ | −302 | −4 |
  | square_bits 27 | 0.882 | −72 | −1 |
  | product_bits 27 | 0.920 | −90 | −2 |
  | result_bits 28 | 0.656 | −88 | −1 (and −1 rotation) |
  | best combo found: (14,17,27,27,27) | 0.953 | **−330** | −4 |
  | safer combo: (13,17,28,27,28) | 0.847 | −288 | −2 |

  So the *unchanged* architecture can shed ~4 % of its Toffolis just by trimming widths, and ~8 % if a better
  polynomial frees `coef_bits = 16`.
* **Low phase bits**: truncating (simply not applying) the j lowest `Z_POW`s of `y1` costs tolerance at large x:
  drop 2 → 0.552, drop 3 → 0.658, drop 4 → 0.838, drop 5 → 1.26 ✗. Each dropped bit saves one rotation (6.8 Toffoli-eq)
  but shrinks `min_margin`, raising all other rotations' cost; net ≈ −17 Toffoli-eq for 3 bits. Small.

## 5. Things to remember when building

* No Hadamard in the gate set: every value is a computational-basis function of `x`, phases only via `Z_POW`
  (hence no phase-gradient tricks); `HMR` is only for measurement-based uncomputation of Z-basis functions and its
  kickback must be fixed with `Z/CZ/CCZ/NEG`. `R` on a |1⟩ randomises the phase → every ancilla must be exactly 0.
* `Q = circuit.num_qubits` counts distinct ids ever mentioned, so `alloc_qubits`/`free` discipline (reuse ids) is
  what matters, not peak width.
* Rotation cost is only 3 % of C; do not spend Toffolis to save rotations unless the trade is < 6.8 Toffolis each.
* Smoke-test with `scripts/verify.sh invsqrt <file> --max-x 4096` (seconds), then the full run (~4 min); only the
  full run's `score` may be quoted.

### Findings

* **Candidate #1 clears the bar.** Degree 2 with k = 8 (192 occurring pieces of the 256 addresses) passes with
  max err/tol 0.65–0.76 for every intermediate width wc ≥ 20 and result width wp ∈ {28, 29, 30}; no x exceeds
  0.8 tol and ~1 % exceed 0.5 tol. k = 9 (384 pieces) passes at 0.14–0.29 but the extra 2 × 272 lookup Toffolis
  cost more than they save in widths. k = 7 and degree 3 at k = 5 fail (float error 2^-24.7 / 2^-25 leaves nothing
  for truncation). Degree 3 at k = 6–7 passes but costs 1,800–2,200 Toffolis per direction, 40–60 % more than
  degree 2 / k = 8, and needs ~25 more qubits.
* **`delta` cannot be truncated**: d must equal 22 − k (every bit of `x_norm` below the address must enter the
  polynomial). Dropping one bit sends max err/tol to 4.6 and 89 % of x above 0.8 — the missing bit is a 2^-20
  relative perturbation of M, i.e. 2^-21 of 1/√M, far above 2^-24. (The reference gets away with 14 of 20 delta
  bits only because Newton squares the error away.)
* **The intermediate width is the knob**: wc = 22 → 20 saves ~80 Toffolis per direction and 4 qubits for +0.03–0.08
  of tolerance; wc = 18 fails (2.13) and wc = 18 at k = 9 overflows the r1 register. wp = 28 instead of 29 saves one
  rotation (6.8 Toffoli-eq) and 28 Toffolis per direction for +0.05 of tolerance.
* **Where the margin goes**: the float quadratic error at k = 8 is 2^-27.7 (≈ 0.08 tol); the rest of the 0.68–0.76
  is the floor-per-row truncation noise of 14 rows (±7 ulp of 2^-wp ≈ 2^-26 ≈ 0.25 tol at wp = 29) plus r1's
  truncation damped by delta ≤ 2^-6. Rounding instead of flooring the rows is not available for free in `_add_row`,
  but the per-piece `c0` already absorbs the mean bias.

### Recommendation: build degree 2, k = 8, wc = 22, wp = 28, d = 14

* Model: max err/tol **0.735** (x = 263,159), 1.0 % of x above 0.5 tol, none above 0.8 — comparable to the

## 10. Signed-digit Horner design (public ideas, our implementation) — 2026-10-24

`docs/public_sota.md` summarises the public Kaggle notebooks. The ideas below are theirs; the code in
`tasks/invsqrt/model_sd.py` and `tasks/invsqrt/build_sd.py` is written from scratch by us. Credits: **Sumit
Chongder** (centred signed-digit Horner with Gidney adders, binary normalisation with a parity-indexed QROM,
coefficient fitting, measurement-cleared flags), **anazemcev / Artem1410** (phase-and-release the static high

part, top-carry reuse, early static-high phase checkpoint), **kirilltym** (integration and independent
verification), **Alex Chilton** (temp-AND moves into still-|0⟩ shifter qubits, shared prefix flags, one
coefficient bit fewer, the cost anatomy that pointed at our `_add_row` rows). Reported dead ends we did not
repeat: cubic × 32 segments, 64 or 256 segments, phase-gradient register, in-place Cuccaro adder, spending
margin on rotations.

### What changes versus §7–§9

* **Rows without partial products.** With centred digits sig_i = 2·x[i] − 1 ∈ {±1}, u = Σ sig_i 2^(i−DU) =
  M − M_mid, every Horner row is one *unconditional* adder of the shifted operand: the sign is applied by
  CNOT-conjugating the target from the digit qubit (~(~t + a) = t − a), the bit of the operand just below the
  cut is the adder's carry-in (round-to-nearest for free). A row costs n − 1 Toffolis for an n-bit target
  and no scratch register. Our `_add_row` rows cost ≈ 2n − 1 plus 29 scratch qubits.
* **Binary normalisation to M ∈ [1,2)**: five stages (shift 16/8/4/2/1); the stage flags are the leading-zero
  count in binary, f0 is the parity that picks one of two 128-segment tables (256 QROM entries addressed by
  x[14:21] + f0). Un-normalising clears each AND flag by an X-basis measurement whose kickback is undone by a
  conditioned multi-controlled Z, which runs on half the shots (the verifier counts executed Toffolis).
* **Phase additivity**: Y = L + 2^S·H with H a per-segment constant from the QROM; H is shifted, phased and
  erased (`del_lookup` of 8 bits) before the arithmetic, so the rows touch only the 21-bit L.
* **Narrow registers**: R = base·2^11 + residual with base looked up (7 bits) and the inner rows on a 12-bit
  residual, merged by one 7-bit adder; last carry computed straight into the target MSB (n − 2 ancillas);
  x[21] (always 1 after normalisation, never read) flipped to 0 and lent to the adders as a carry wire.
